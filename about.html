About KFC

KFC (Kentucky Fried Chicken) is a global fast-food chain known for its fried chicken. Founded in 1930 by Colonel Sanders, KFC has become one of the most recognizable fast-food brands in the world.

Our Story

KFC was founded in 1930 by Colonel Harland Sanders in Corbin, Kentucky. He developed a unique recipe for fried chicken that became the foundation of the KFC brand.

Our Menu

  • Original Recipe Chicken
  • Extra Crispy Chicken
  • Grilled Chicken
  • Chicken Tenders
  • Side Dishes

Our Goal

Our goal is to provide delicious, high-quality food and excellent customer service to all our patrons. We strive to be the best fast-food restaurant in the world.
